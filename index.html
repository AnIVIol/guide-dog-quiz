🦮 Guide Dog Quiz
🔒 Admin settings
Dog photos (upload & resize)

Upload a picture for each dog. Images keep their original proportions and scale to fit the screen.

Sound effects

Pick sounds for each moment of the quiz. Add several to a moment and a random one plays each time. Visitors can mute everything with the speaker button at the top of the page.

Add, edit or remove dogs

Use 2 to 8 dogs. Removing a dog moves its answers to a neighboring dog. Adding, removing or resetting returns you to the start screen.

Edit questions, answers & fun facts

Changes save automatically in this browser. Adding, deleting or resetting questions returns you to the start screen.

Question & fun-fact pictures

Replace the emoji banner on any question or fun-fact screen with your own picture. Images keep their original proportions and scale to fit the screen.

Fun facts come from the Samsung Fire & Marine Insurance Guide Dog School website, guidedog.co.kr, and are the school’s own statements.
